The week plans itself

Seven days of breakfast, lunch, and dinner from your household's Food Profile — veg, eggetarian, or non-veg, North or South Indian.

02

Your cook sees only today

A calm Daily Cook View with step-by-step Recipe Guides, readable or read aloud in English or Hindi.

03

The cart builds itself

Missing ingredients flagged by the cook become a Suggested Cart. You approve, check out on Instamart, done.

04

One chat, nothing lost

Text, photos, and voice notes between family and cook. Plan changes and requests post themselves.

"Didi, dhaniya khatam."

When the cook runs out of something, they just say it — typed or spoken, in their own words. It lands in one queue for you to review, and the Suggested Cart quietly adds it to the next order.
