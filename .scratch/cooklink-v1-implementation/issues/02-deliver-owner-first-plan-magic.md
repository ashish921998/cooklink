# 02 — Deliver the Owner's first-plan magic

**What to build:** Let a new person sign in by phone, create a Household with
only the minimum planning inputs, and reach a useful Member Today experience
with an active seven-day plan before being asked to invite anyone.

**Blocked by:** 01 — Finish the durable repository foundation.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A phone-verified new person can create a Household and becomes its
      Household Owner without a client-supplied role being trusted.
- [ ] Setup asks only for Household identity, Serving Count, Meal Style, and
      Vegetarian/Eggetarian/Non-vegetarian preference; optional preferences are
      skippable.
- [ ] Generation progress appears immediately after the minimum inputs are
      submitted.
- [ ] A complete seven-day breakfast/lunch/dinner plan becomes active within
      15 seconds on a normal network, or a safe starter plan activates while
      personalization continues.
- [ ] The Owner lands on Today and can open the complete Weekly Meal Plan.
- [ ] A failed or interrupted setup resumes safely without creating duplicate
      Households or conflicting Owner memberships.
- [ ] Server tests prove the new Household and plan are inaccessible to another
      authenticated person.
