# 03 — Invite Members and Cooks into role-aware navigation

**What to build:** Let the Household Owner securely invite Members and Cooks
through WhatsApp, then give each accepted participant the correct, simple entry
experience while supporting one account that is a Member at home and a Cook
elsewhere.

**Blocked by:** 02 — Deliver the Owner's first-plan magic.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The Owner can create, revoke, and resend a phone-bound, role-specific,
      single-use Household Invite that expires after seven days.
- [ ] Only a person verified with the matching phone number can accept the
      invite.
- [ ] Acceptance enforces one Household Role per person, at most two active
      Cooks per Household, and at most 30 active Households per Cook.
- [ ] A Member launches into Today with Today, Meal Plan, and Groceries as the
      persistent Household destinations.
- [ ] A Cook launches into the Household list even when exactly one Household
      is available, and selecting a Household opens Chat.
- [ ] A person with both kinds of membership can move between My home and Work
      households, and the app remembers the last area used.
- [ ] Switching Household context never retains another Household's identity,
      content, unread state, draft state, or permissions.
- [ ] Removed or revoked access immediately exits protected Household content
      with a plain explanation.
